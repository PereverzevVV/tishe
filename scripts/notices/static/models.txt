Speech and language models
==========================

Tishe does not ship any speech recognition model or language model in its
installer. The user chooses and downloads models inside the app, from
Hugging Face or from the Handy model mirror (blob.handy.computer). Each model
stays under the license of its authors. The notices below give the
attributions those licenses ask for.

The installer ships one model file:

- Silero VAD v6.2 (resources/models/silero-vad-v6.2-F32.gguf), voice
  activity detection. Copyright (c) 2020-present Silero Team. MIT License.
  https://github.com/snakers4/silero-vad
- GigaAM vocabulary (resources/models/gigaam_vocab.txt), from GigaAM v3 by
  SberDevices (ai-sage/GigaAM-v3). MIT License.

The models below are used as published, converted to GGUF or ONNX format
without changes to their weights, except where a quantized file is chosen.

- NVIDIA Parakeet (TDT, CTC, RNN-T, TDT-CTC and Unified EN variants) and
  NVIDIA Canary (180M Flash, 1B Flash, 1B v2, Canary-Qwen 2.5B).
  Copyright NVIDIA Corporation. https://huggingface.co/nvidia
- Parakeet Ultra 0.6B by Moondream (moondream/parakeet-ultra), further
  trained from NVIDIA Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3.
- Parakeet TDT 0.6B primeLine (primeline/parakeet-primeline), fine-tuned
  from NVIDIA Parakeet.
- WeSpeaker ResNet34 speaker embedding model trained on VoxCeleb
  (used for speaker separation), by the WeNet community.
  https://github.com/wenet-e2e/wespeaker
